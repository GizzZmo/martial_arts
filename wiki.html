Welcome

This wiki is my map of martial arts literature—libraries, publishers, journals, and primary sources— built from years of chasing footnotes, catalogs, and translations. It is written for practitioners, researchers, and anyone who wants checkable sources rather than marketing noise.

Navigate the sections below. For a longer, more personal account of why this project exists, read Words from the Author.
